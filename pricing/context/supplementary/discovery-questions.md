# Discovery Questions

Use as prompts, not a script. For each answer, ask for the most recent real example, including exceptions and workarounds. Best technique: ask the operator to screen-share the last disputed invoice, last container-size change, last unmatched payment, and last rolloff overage.

Legend: ★ product tradeoff; ◆ trust boundary (what they would never let software do alone).

## Residential
- ★ Walk me through how you priced the last new residential account, from base rate through fees, taxes, discounts, and exceptions.
- ★ Which residential customers pay something different from the standard rate, and how did those exceptions arise?
- ★ ◆ Between accepting a customer and starting service, which decisions require human judgment, and which would you never trust software to make automatically?
- ★ What events cause a residential bill to differ from the normal recurring amount, and how do you verify those charges before sending?
- How do customers pay today, and what problems occur with autopay, failed payments, refunds, or payment application?
- ★ How do you decide when to remind, charge a late fee, suspend, retrieve a cart, or write off?
- ★ Think of the last customer who could not complete signup cleanly. What blocked them?
- When someone cancels or upgrades mid-quarter, what do you actually do with the money? Is "no proration" policy or a system limitation?
- Do you charge a fuel or environmental surcharge? If not, is that a marketing choice against the nationals?
- How do HOA and municipal contract accounts get billed differently, and how many households sit behind one bill?

## Commercial frontload
- ★ Walk me through the last commercial quote. How did size, frequency, weight, contamination, distance, term, and competitive pressure affect price?
- ★ When costs change, do you raise the base rate, add a surcharge, renegotiate, or leave the price?
- ★ After a customer accepts a quote, how do sales, ops, dispatch, and billing agree on what was sold and when billing begins?
- ★ ◆ Which commercial charges or adjustments would you never trust software to approve or send without review?
- How do POs, consolidated payments, credits, short pays, and multi-location payments affect cash application?
- ★ When a commercial account is delinquent, what determines the next action?
- ★ Show me one commercial contract. Where does that price live today, and what happens when your rate card goes up 5%?
- How do you handle a customer with 15 sites: one invoice, fifteen, or a statement?
- Which frontload extras do you routinely waive?
- Do you have auto-renewal and notice windows, and how do you track renewal dates?

## Rolloff
- ★ For the last rolloff order, how did you price delivery, rental days, haul, disposal, weight, material, distance, prohibited-material fees?
- ★ ◆ Which parts of scheduling, container selection, site qualification, or authorization would you never let software handle alone?
- ★ What evidence do you need before billing a rolloff charge, and what happens when it is missing?
- How do payment expectations differ among one-time customers, contractors, brokers, and account customers?
- ★ When a rolloff invoice is disputed or overdue, what determines whether you hold the next delivery?
- ★ Is your pricing all-in with a tonnage cap, or haul plus disposal, and does that differ between homeowners and contractors?
- When the scale ticket comes back over the cap, what happens next, in order, and how long until you get paid?
- When does rental time stop: customer request, scheduled pickup, actual removal, or disposal?

## Portable toilet, septic, scale
- For unit rentals, how do you bill a unit placed on the 20th, and do you have monthly minimums? Calendar month or 28-day cycle?
- At the scale house, how do cash and account tickets get into billing, and what goes wrong?

## Workflow, payments, data
- ★ Where does billing data live today (QB Desktop or Online, spreadsheets, legacy), what syncs, and what do you re-key?
- ★ Walk me through your last billing run: how long, what did you catch before sending, what got caught after?
- How do you post checks and split processor deposits, and how many hours a month does reconciliation take?
- ★ Which taxes and franchise fees apply to which line items, and who figured that out?
- What is your suspension and reinstatement policy, and how does the driver know not to service a suspended stop?

## Signup and portal
- ★ If you could publish prices online, would you? What would stop you?
- What do customers call about most, and which could a portal or an AI agent absorb without upsetting anyone?
- When a customer disputes an invoice, what evidence do you reach for?
