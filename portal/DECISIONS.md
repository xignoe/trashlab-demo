# Decisions (portal surface)

Every place the portal build had to choose between two reasonable readings of SHARED_CONTRACT.md, and why.

1. **Types transcribed from prose.** The contract describes src/types.ts in shorthand rather than shipping a file. The portal transcribes it field for field, with no additions, so the merge can diff it against the contract text.
2. **Paper tokens are neutral.** The Paper MCP was reachable, but the open file ("Forgiving kale") had zero artboards and zero tokens, so there was nothing to extract. Per the contract's fallback, tokens.css is a neutral set and DESIGN.md says so.
3. **Manager did not write product code.** Per MANAGER.md, the manager wrote CHECKLIST.md, scripts/build_dashboard.py, and this file; phases were built by fresh subagents.

Later entries are appended by the phase subagents.

4. **cat_res_extra_pickup added to the catalog.** The contract catalog has no priced line for a one-off residential extra pickup, but the portal's extra pickup flow must quote it through resolvePrice and computeCharge. Added `cat_res_extra_pickup` (residential, "Extra pickup", unit cart, public true) with a published zone_open RateVersion of 2500 effective 2026-01-01 and a superseded 2200 version effective 2025-01-01. It has no frequency, so resolvePrice matches it on catalog and zone alone.
5. **Vacation hold policy constants live in the engine.** Hauler.policy has no hold fields (only proration, late fee, grace, suspension, reinstatement). The portal keeps `HOLD_POLICY = { minDays: 7, maxDays: 90 }` in src/store/engine.ts (Phase 4) rather than adding fields to the Hauler type, and the hold date range goes into a portal-local `holds` table since ServiceItem has no hold dates.
6. **Maple blocked event added for the missed pickup scenario.** The contract only names Maple's extraBags event. The portal seeds four Monday events for site_maple: 2026-08-17 completed with extraBags and photo, 2026-08-24 missed ("Stop skipped, truck full, no photo"), 2026-08-31 completed with photo, and 2026-09-07 blocked with the driver note, a photo, and driver "R. Alvarez". The blocked event is what the "report a missed pickup and see the photo" scenario exercises; the missed event is what creates a recovery work order.
7. **Maple partial payment derivation.** inv_maple_2026q3 carries three quarterly recurring charges computed exactly as computeCharge would: 96 gal cart base 8700, fuel 609, env 100, tax 652 (7% of 9309), total 10061; extra cart base 2700, fuel 189, env 100, tax 202, total 3191; recycling base 3600, fuel 252, env 100, tax 270, total 4222; plus a lateFee charge of 1000 with no fees and no tax. Invoice total 18474. The contract says Maple is past due 8745, so the seed has one settled card payment `pay_card_maple_partial` of 9729 (18474 minus 8745) allocated to that invoice on 2026-07-28. Open balance is therefore exactly 8745, which check_seed.mjs asserts.
8. **ServiceEvent.date carries a timestamp.** The contract says dates are ISO 8601 strings and Phase 5 needs "Our driver completed this stop at <time>", but ServiceEvent has no separate time field. Seeded events use a full ISO timestamp in `date` (for example 2026-09-07T07:51:00). Anything that matches an event to a calendar day must compare `date.slice(0, 10)`; `eventForSiteOn` in Phase 2 will do this. The missed event uses T00:00:00 because there is no service time to report.
9. **Photo paths are relative to src/seed.** Seeded `photoUrl` values look like `photos/blocked-driveway.svg`. `src/seed/index.ts` exports `resolvePhoto()` which maps those to Vite-served URLs via `import.meta.glob`, so the SVGs live in src/seed/photos as the checklist asks and still ship in the production build without a copy step.
10. **Oakridge invoices bill in arrears.** acct_pm_oakridge has billedInAdvance false and cycle net30, so inv_oak_2026_09 (issued 2026-09-01, due 2026-10-01) covers service for 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-31, and the three paid invoices cover May through July. Each invoice has one recurring charge per site with that site's PO number in the description; each monthly invoice totals 91992 and the single check pay_chk_oakridge for 275976 is allocated across the three older ones.
11. **batch_0908 uses 13 full payments plus one partial.** Every generic residential account has one current invoice (monthly September or quarterly Q3) so batch payments can be allocated rather than left floating. No 14-subset of those invoice totals sums to the contract's 131842 gross, so the generator picks 13 invoices paid in full and one invoice paid 48 cents short (inv_res_028_current, 17426 of 17474), which reads as a customer typo and leaves a small open balance for the office to see. Fees are 4120 and net 127722 as specified.
12. **Late fee ruleWon is standardRate.** The late fee charge is policy-driven, not priced from a RateVersion or contract, and the ruleWon union has no policy value. It is recorded with `ruleWon: 'standardRate'`, no rateVersionId, and `source: { type: 'manual', id: 'hauler_piedmont' }` so the provenance drawer can point at hauler policy.
13. **Holt's hold is seeded as an open request.** acct_res_holt has status hold, its service item is held, and the seeded vacationHold Request is status open with the requested range in its note, since the checklist asks for one open vacationHold Request and Phase 4's holds table does not exist yet.
14. **Kerr is on route_tue_res.** The contract does not name Kerr's route. Placing the suspended account on Tuesday means route_mon_res keeps its spare capacity for the extra pickup scenario and the skippedSuspended events fall on 08-18, 08-25, 09-01, and 09-08.
15. **Build fix: @types/node.** `vite.config.ts` reads `process.env.PORT` so the running dev server can keep port 5175 while a second one runs elsewhere. `tsc -b` failed on that reference, so `@types/node` is a devDependency and `tsconfig.node.json` lists `"types": ["node"]`. No product code changed.
16. **The engine reads bound tables.** The contract engine signatures take no state argument, yet a portal mutation (a new work order, a new allocation) has to be visible to the next `resolvePrice`, `computeCharge`, or `allocate` call. `engine.ts` reads through `tables()`, which defaults to the raw seed; `useStore.ts` calls `bindTables(() => useStore.getState())` once at load so the live store wins in the app. Tests bind a fixture or the seed. Signatures are untouched.
17. **computeCharge takes optional `frequency` and `description`.** The contract signature has no frequency, but the pricing block must come from `resolvePrice`, which needs one for frequency-specific rate versions. Both are optional additions; every contract argument keeps its name and meaning. When `catalogId` is absent (a late fee) the pricing block is `{ ruleWon: 'standardRate' }` with no rateVersionId, matching entry 12.
18. **Contract overrides apply only inside the term.** `resolvePrice` skips a contract whose termStart..termEnd does not cover onDate, so a lapsed contract falls through to the zone rate. An override with no frequency matches any frequency; one with a frequency must match exactly. A RateVersion with no frequency (extra pickup, roll off) likewise matches any requested frequency.
19. **Portal-minted ids carry a `_p` infix.** `newId('req')` yields `req_p0001`. Seeded ids never use that infix, so store rows created in the browser cannot collide with the seed and the log makes them easy to spot.
20. **Log timestamps are TODAY plus the wall clock.** Every date in the portal agrees on 2026-09-10, so `log[].at` and `Payment.receivedAt` for new rows use `nowISO()` = `2026-09-10T<hh:mm:ss>` from the real clock. Ordering is preserved without leaking the real date.
21. **Locked and posted rows are guarded in the store, not just by convention.** `updateInvoice` throws on `locked`, `updateCharge` throws on `status posted`, and `addCharge` refuses a charge whose status is already `posted` (posting is an office action). `useStore.test.ts` proves the invoice guard. There is no delete method on any table, which is how invariant 5 (waived rows are never removed) holds.
22. **Cycle math lives in the engine now.** The Overview's next invoice estimate needs `nextCycleStart(account, fromDate)`, `cycleEnd`, and `nextInvoicePeriod`, so they ship in Phase 2 with unit tests; Phase 4 can reuse them for the cart change flow instead of adding a second helper. For accounts billed in advance (Maple, quarterly) the next invoice covers the cycle that starts next (2026-10-01 to 2026-12-31). For Oakridge (net30, arrears, entry 10) the invoice issued 2026-10-01 covers the month that is running now (2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30). The estimate base is monthly rate times months in cycle times qty, run through `computeCharge` so fees and tax match what a real charge would carry; Maple's Q4 estimate reproduces the seeded Q3 recurring total exactly (17474) and each Oakridge site reproduces its seeded monthly charge.
23. **Open items are requests with status open or scheduled.** The Overview count and the Phase 4 list both use `openItemsForAccount`, which spans every site on the account. Done and declined requests are history and do not count.
24. **Only two logins in the switcher.** The "Signed in as" select lists Dana Maple and Oakridge Property Management. `switchAccount` accepts any seeded account so a test can sign in as acct_res_kerr for the suspended-account scenario; if that happens the switcher shows the current account as a third option rather than going blank.
25. **Portal-local `paymentMethods` table.** The contract only records `paymentMethodOnFile: 'card' | 'ach'` on BillingAccount, which is not enough to show "Visa ending in 4242" or to charge a saved method. `useStore.ts` carries a portal-local `paymentMethods` table, one row per account: `{ accountId, kind, last4, tokenId, brand, savedAt }`. It is seeded in the store (not in src/seed, which is the shared contract) for Maple (card 4242), Oakridge (ach 6710), Holt (card 1881), and the roll off homeowner (card 0093), matching each account's `paymentMethodOnFile`. `savePaymentMethod` replaces the row and rewrites `paymentMethodOnFile` so the two never disagree. The table never holds a card number, expiry, CVC, or bank account number; `savePaymentMethod` refuses any tokenId that does not start with `tok_` and any last4 that is not four digits.
26. **Payment is mock tokenized end to end.** `src/lib/paymentToken.ts` plays the processor's hosted field: `tokenizeMockMethod(kind)` returns `{ tokenId: 'tok_xxx', kind, last4, brand }` and nothing else. `HostedField.tsx` renders a labelled placeholder and a "Use a test card" button; there is no text input for card or bank data anywhere in src. The pay sheet's only free text input is the dollar amount.
27. **`recordPayment` is one store method.** The pay flow needs a Payment, its allocations, and the status flip to happen together. `recordPayment({ accountId, method, invoiceIds, cents })` runs `allocate` first (so an over-allocation throws before anything is written), then `addPayment` (status settled, receivedAt from `nowISO()`), then `addAllocations`, then `refreshAccountStatus`. The log therefore shows `addPayment`, `addAllocations`, and, when it changed, `accountStatus:pastDue->active`.
28. **Account status is re-derived from invoices, but only between active and pastDue.** `refreshAccountStatus` sets pastDue when any invoice has an open balance and a dueAt before TODAY, active otherwise. Suspended and hold are office decisions (reinstatement fee, hold range) and are never touched by a portal payment; Kerr stays suspended even if a test pays its balance.
29. **Invoice status is computed, not stored.** `invoiceStatus(state, id, TODAY)` is paid when the open balance is zero, pastDue when something is open after dueAt, open otherwise. Oakridge's September invoice (due 2026-10-01) is open, not past due, so Oakridge stays active with a $919.92 balance.
30. **Pay now pays one invoice.** The sheet is opened from a specific invoice and allocates to it alone; the amount defaults to that invoice's open balance and is clamped to [min(100, open), open]. A balance under $1.00 (batch_0908 leaves inv_res_028_current 48 cents short, entry 11) can only be paid in full. Paying with a new token defaults to saving it as the method on file, which replaces the existing row.
31. **Autopay on requires a saved method.** With no `paymentMethods` row the toggle opens the method sheet with an explanatory line, and autopay turns on only after a token is saved. Turning off shows the earliest open invoice's due date; with nothing open it names the next issue date from `nextCycleStart`.
32. **The provenance drawer recomputes nothing.** "Why this charge" reads baseCents, fees[], taxCents, totalCents, pricing, and source off the stored Charge and looks up the referenced RateVersion, Contract, FeeRule, TaxRule, ServiceItem, ServiceEvent, and ScaleTicket rows for display. The taxable amount shown is base (when the tax rule applies to the line type) plus fees whose rule is taxable, which is how computeCharge built it. The "unit x months x qty = base" line appears only when that product equals the stored base; otherwise the base is shown as a plain amount. Late fee lines cite hauler policy (lateFeeCents, lateFeeDay) in steps 1 and 2 and say outright in steps 3 and 4 that no fee and no tax apply.
33. **Addendum D5 now governs entry 4.** cat_res_extra_pickup is retired. Extra pickup is an event exception priced from src/seed/eventRates.json, and its Charge is lineType event with source type manual. Entry 4 stays as history.
34. **Addendum C5 now governs entries 7 and 22.** Flat fees apply once per whole month in a charge's period, so quarterly lines carry fee_env_1 at 300 and Maple's Q3 96 gal line totals 10261. The invoice total and the partial payment in entry 7 change, but the 8745 past due balance does not. Entry 22's 17474 estimate is superseded by the regenerated figure.
35. **Addendum B1, B2, and C8 correct Phase 1 seed and types.** Escalator anniversaries become full ISO dates, Invoice.deliveredVia becomes the shared union, and a zone_franchise TaxRule is added. No earlier entry recorded these choices.
36. **OWNERSHIP.md now governs entry 5 and the Phase 4 and 5 write boxes.** Account owns ServiceItem and account status, and storefront and pricing own Quote. The vacation hold, cart change, and commercial quote flows therefore write only portal-local tables and Requests, with the owned writes stubbed behind named functions. The Phase 3 autopay and status writes on BillingAccount predate OWNERSHIP.md and stay as built. The merge decides whether they route through account.

37. **Vacation hold stubs the ServiceItem write.** `placeHold` writes a portal-local `holds` row per active service item and a Request of kind vacationHold, status scheduled. The ServiceItem status change and any BillingAccount hold status are account's writes (OWNERSHIP.md), so the portal calls `requestServiceItemHold()`, a store method that only appends `stub:requestServiceItemHold` with the item ids and range to the log. ServiceItem.status and BillingAccount.status are never touched by the hold flow. A service item with a hold row whose end is today or later cannot be held again (`holdableServiceItems`).
38. **Cart change stubs the ServiceItem write in a `pendingChanges` table.** `changeCart` writes a Request of kind cartChange, status scheduled, whose note carries the effective date; a WorkOrder of kind swap, status scheduled, on the next route day, pointing at the current ServiceItem and container; and then calls `proposeCartChange()`, which records `{ id, serviceItemId, fromCatalogId, toCatalogId, effectiveFrom, requestId, workOrderId }` in the portal-local `pendingChanges` table. It never ends or adds a ServiceItem; account does that from the Request (OWNERSHIP.md). The Overview reads `pendingChanges` for the "Pending change" card and prices the next invoice estimate at the new size once the change is effective for that period. One pending change per cart. The portal-minted id prefix is `chgreq_p`.
39. **Paid extra pickup charges are approved, following addendum C13.** Storefront writes prepaid first-cycle Charges as approved (C13). The portal does the same for a paid extra pickup: the Charge from computeCharge is status approved, lineType event, source `{ type: 'manual', id: 'eventRates.extraPickup' }`, no catalogId, ruleWon standardRate (C11: a table rate, not a hand-set amount). The settled Payment is recorded unallocated because no invoice exists yet; billing posts the charge on the next run and allocates the payment then, the same merge task C13 names.
40. **Extra pickup base comes from eventRates.json.** `src/seed/eventRates.json` carries billing's canonical values (extraBags 250, overload 1000, contamination 2000, dryRun 2500) plus the portal-local `extraPickup: 2500`, and `src/seed/index.ts` exports it as `eventRates` beside the seed tables (it is a rate table, not a contract entity, so it is not in `Seed`). check_seed.mjs asserts the four canonical values and that cat_res_extra_pickup and its rate versions are gone.
41. **Reseed moved the batch_0908 partial.** With the per-month env fee (addendum C5) every quarterly generic invoice grew by 200 per service line, so the generator's search now lands on 13 full payments plus 18072 of inv_res_028_current's 18074, two cents short rather than the 48 in entry 11. Gross, fees, and net are unchanged (131842, 4120, 127722). Maple's Q3 invoice is now 19074 (10261 + 3391 + 4422 + 1000) and pay_card_maple_partial is 10329, so the open balance stays exactly 8745 (entry 7 and 34).

42. **Missed pickup reads the driver record before filing anything.** `missedPickupLookup(state, siteId, date, TODAY)` classifies a report in order: inside the window, the site has a route, the date is a route day, then the ServiceEvent. A blocked outcome or a notOut exception wins over completed, since a notOut stop was never serviced. Completed and blocked write nothing unless the customer asks the office or disputes (an open missedPickup Request carrying their note). Missed runs `reportMissedPickup`, which writes a scheduled missedPickup Request and a recovery WorkOrder on `nextBusinessDay10am(TODAY)` (Friday 2026-09-11), pointing at the site's first active ServiceItem and container. Skipped for suspension and no record end in the HandoffCard with an open Request. Every missed pickup note carries the ISO date, and `missedPickupReportFor` uses it so the same site and day is never filed twice. A lookup is run on submit, not on every date change, so browsing the picker files nothing.
43. **The missed pickup window is 14 days, TODAY included.** Hauler.policy has no reporting window, so `MISSED_PICKUP_WINDOW_DAYS = 14` lives in engine.ts beside HOLD_POLICY, and the date input's min and max come from `missedPickupWindow(TODAY)` (2026-08-27 to 2026-09-10). This puts Maple's seeded missed event on 2026-08-24 outside the window (entry 6), so on seed data the missed path is reachable only in tests; see "Requests for shared/" item 2.
44. **Commercial quote stubs the Quote write in a `quoteRequests` table.** OWNERSHIP.md: storefront and pricing write Quote, so the portal never adds to `quotes`. `requestQuote` mints the Request id first, calls `submitQuoteRequest()`, which records `{ id, quote, accountId, siteId, material, accessNotes?, requestId, followUpBy }` in the portal-local `quoteRequests` table and logs `stub:submitQuoteRequest`, then writes a Request of kind quote, status open, whose note names the quote id. The draft is Quote-shaped: kind commercialRequest, status draft, createdVia `agent` (the union has no portal value), one line with catalogId, qty, frequency, priceCents 0, dueTodayCents 0, recurringCents 0, expiresAt TODAY plus 30 days at 23:59:59 (the seed's format). Material and access notes have no Quote field, so they sit on the quoteRequests row for pricing. followUpBy is `nextBusinessDay10am(TODAY)`, the same promise the HandoffCard makes.
45. **Quote size and material map onto the frontload catalog.** Sizes are the distinct frontload sizeLabels (2 yd, 3 yd). Wood waste uses the wood waste item of that size when one exists (cat_fl_3yd_wood); every other pairing, including 2 yd wood waste, uses the plain item of that size and the material rides on the quote request.
46. **The picker offers only what can succeed.** Request a quote shows only for non-homeowner payers. Cart size change shows only when a site on the account has an active cat_res_96 or cat_res_64 ServiceItem (`accountHasResidentialCart`), so Oakridge never sees it and never files a handoff Request just by opening it. Switching site closes every flow except the quote form, which stays open and takes the new site as its default.

47. **The missed pickup window is 21 days (supersedes entry 43 and "Requests for shared/" item 2).** The 14 day limit was a manager checklist choice, not a contract rule, and it put Maple's seeded 2026-08-24 missed event out of reach, so scenario 2 could not create a recovery work order on seed data. Phase 6 raises `MISSED_PICKUP_WINDOW_DAYS` in src/store/engine.ts to 21. The window is now 2026-08-20 to 2026-09-10 and 2026-08-24 books a recovery on Friday 2026-09-11. This is portal-local (Hauler.policy has no reporting window), so nothing is asked of shared/ and the seed is unchanged.
48. **Portal tokens alias the canonical TrashLab tokens (addendum F2).** src/styles/tl-tokens.css is a copy of ../shared/tokens.css without its trailing Tailwind v4 `@theme` block (the portal runs Tailwind 3). src/styles/tokens.css keeps every portal variable name and points it at a `--tl-` value, so component code and tailwind.config.js did not change. Manrope and IBM Plex Mono load from Google Fonts in index.html. The status pill takes Paper's pattern (ink text, colored dot) in the `.tl-pill` class. DESIGN.md lists the mapping and the drift.
49. **Invariants panel checks 2, 4, 5, and 6.** src/lib/invariants.ts runs pure checks over live store state and the Overview footer renders them. Invariant 4 is checked as "no Charge and no Payment for an account whose status is suspended", which is stricter than the contract wording (never charges) and matches the Phase 6 checklist. Invariant 5 is proved by the store exposing no delete, remove, drop, or purge method and every seeded waived row being present. Invariants 1, 3, and 7 are billing and office runs the portal never performs, so the panel names them as out of scope rather than showing a pass it cannot earn.

2. **Withdrawn (entry 47).** ~~A missed event inside the 14 day window, or a wider window.~~ Resolved portal-local by raising the window to 21 days; the text below is kept as history. The Phase 5 checklist limits the missed pickup date picker to the last 14 days, but the seeded Maple missed event is 2026-08-24, 17 days before TODAY, and the Phase 6 scenario 2 expects that date to create a recovery work order. Either move the missed event to a date inside 2026-08-27..2026-09-10 that is not already used (no free Monday remains for site_maple, so this means changing 2026-08-31 from completed to missed) or raise MISSED_PICKUP_WINDOW_DAYS to 21. The portal keeps 14 until the manager decides.
