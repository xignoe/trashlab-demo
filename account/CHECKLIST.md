## Contract expansion rules (read before Phase 1, apply in every phase)

The contract writes `src/types.ts` in shorthand. Expand it to real TypeScript with these rules and nothing else:
- Every type is `export type` or `export interface`. Field names and union literals are copied exactly. Fields followed by `?` are optional.
- `address`, `serial`, `facility`, `material`, `driver`, `note`, `reason`, `number`, `accessNotes`, `poNumber`, `photoUrl`, `holdReason`, `by`, `name` are `string`. Every `*Cents`, `*Lbs`, `*Pct`, `qty`, `value`, `capacityStops`, `includedTons`, `includedDays`, `renewalNoticeDays`, `lateFeeDay`, `graceMissedPickups`, `suspendAfterDays`, `pct` is `number`. Every `*At`, `*From`, `*To`, `date`, `servicedOn`, `scheduledFor`, `termStart`, `termEnd`, `holdDeadline`, `expiresAt` is `string` (ISO 8601). `period` is `{ start: string; end: string }`.
- `Contract.escalator.anniversary` is a `string` in `MM-DD` form (the contract says "4% on Jan 1", so `"01-01"`). Superseded by addendum B1: a full ISO date such as `"2027-01-01"`.
- `Zone.publicPricing`, `ServiceCatalog.public`, `BillingAccount.autopay`, `BillingAccount.billedInAdvance`, `BillingAccount.taxExempt`, `FeeRule.taxable`, `Invoice.locked` are `boolean`.
- Add nothing to types.ts beyond the contract. Surface-only view models live in `src/store/selectors.ts`, never in types.ts.
- Seed lives in `src/seed/*.json`, one file per entity (kebab until polish; addendum D3 renames to camelCase plural in Phase 7) (plural, kebab: `haulers.json`, `parties.json`, `billing-accounts.json`, `sites.json`, `zones.json`, `routes.json`, `service-catalog.json`, `containers.json`, `service-items.json`, `rate-versions.json`, `fee-rules.json`, `tax-rules.json`, `contracts.json`, `quotes.json`, `work-orders.json`, `service-events.json`, `scale-tickets.json`, `charges.json`, `waived-charges.json`, `invoices.json`, `credit-memos.json`, `payments.json`, `payment-allocations.json`, `processor-batches.json`, `requests.json`) plus `src/seed/index.ts` that imports them all and exports one typed `SeedData` object.
- Rounding: cents are rounded half up with `Math.round` at the point a fee or tax is computed. A flat fee applies once per charge line per month in the charge period (a quarterly recurring line carries three months of a $1 fee).
- Log every judgment call in `DECISIONS.md` with the two options and why.
