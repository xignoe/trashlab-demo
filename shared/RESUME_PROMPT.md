# Paste this into each surface agent before it continues

Before dispatching your next phase, do the following and nothing else first:

1. Read shared/README.md, shared/CONTRACT_ADDENDUM.md, and shared/OWNERSHIP.md at the repo root (one level above your surface folder).
2. Find every addendum item tagged "next phase" that names your surface. Add each one as a box at the top of your next phase in CHECKLIST.md, prefixed "Addendum:". Items tagged "polish" go into your final phase. Items tagged "merge" are not yours; do not add them.
3. If any earlier decision in your DECISIONS.md is overridden by the addendum, append a short entry to DECISIONS.md saying which item now governs. Do not rewrite history.
4. Check OWNERSHIP.md. If any box in your remaining phases writes an entity or builds a screen your surface does not own, replace that box with a stub box ("stub <thing> behind <function>, note in DECISIONS.md") and continue.
5. Do not touch shared/, prompts/, trashlab/, checkin/, or any other surface folder. Do not dispatch subagents to the Paper MCP; Paper boxes are done by you (the manager) or left unticked with the note "Paper: manager".
6. Then dispatch the next phase exactly as MANAGER.md describes. Use the fixed dev port for your surface from addendum section G, with --strictPort.

Reply with the list of Addendum boxes you added, then proceed.
